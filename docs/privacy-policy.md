# LetterCrack privacy policy

_Draft for https://lettercrack.app/privacy. Last updated: 5 November 2026._

LetterCrack is a puzzle game that works offline. We built it to collect as little as possible.

## What we collect

- **Nothing that identifies you.** There is no account, sign-in or profile.
- **Crash reports.** If the app crashes, it may send a technical report (device model, OS version, app version and the error) to our crash-reporting provider, Sentry. Reports contain no name, email, location or puzzle content.

## What stays on your device

Your progress, streak, hints and settings are stored on your device. On iPhone and iPad they also sync through your own iCloud account, which we cannot read. On Android they are included in your device backup.

## Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

## Reporting a quote

"Report this quote" hides the quote on your device. If you later send us feedback by email, the list of reported quotes is included in the draft so you can see it before you send.

## Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

## Changes

If we add ads or purchases in a future version, we will update this policy before that version is released.
