## Description

Every LetterCrack puzzle is a famous quote in disguise. Each letter has been swapped for another. Work out the swaps, letter by letter, until the words appear.

A NEW QUOTE EVERY DAY
One daily cryptogram, the same for everyone, ready each morning. Missed a day? Every past daily waits in the archive.

SOLVE AT YOUR OWN PACE
No timers. No lives. Nothing to lose. Wrong guesses are never judged until you ask, and you can leave a puzzle and come back to it exactly as you left it.

MEET THE AUTHOR
After every solve you get the full quote, a short note on who wrote it, and the work it comes from. Every quote is public domain, from writers such as Shakespeare, Milton, Franklin and Emerson.

BIG, CLEAR LETTERS
A large custom keyboard, four text sizes on top of your device setting, dark mode and a high-contrast theme. Full VoiceOver support, and an external keyboard works on iPad.

YOUR PROGRESS, BACKED UP
Your streak, solved quotes and settings sync with iCloud. One streak freeze a week covers a missed day.

FEATURES
• A daily quote plus a level path of hundreds more
• Four difficulty levels, from gentle to expert
• Reveal and Check hints when you are stuck
• Fill every matching letter at once, or one at a time
• Gentle highlighting of letters used twice
• Streaks, stats and favorite authors
• Share a solved quote as a card
