# Notes for App Review (2.1)

LetterCrack is an offline cryptogram game. There is no account, login or server. Every quote ships inside the app.

**How to play:** tap a box, then type a letter on the on-screen keyboard. The letter fills every box with the same cipher letter. The puzzle is solved when every letter is right.

**Hints:** "Reveal" fills one letter everywhere; "Check" marks wrong letters. Players get 5 hints a day, refilled at local midnight. This version shows no advertising and offers no in-app purchases.

**No fail state:** there are no lives, energy or countdown timers. An optional solve-time stat can be turned on in Settings.

**Quotes:** all quotes are public domain: every author died before 1956 and every source was published before 1929. Settings › About our quotes lists the sources. "Report this quote" (the ⋯ button on a puzzle) hides a quote on the device. Nothing is sent online.

**Data:** progress syncs through the player's own iCloud key-value store. Optional crash reports (no personal data) may be sent to Sentry. Notifications are only requested if the player turns on the daily reminder.

**To test quickly:** the first launch shows a three-letter tutorial ("A CAT"). Tap Skip to go straight to the difficulty picker.
