## Full description

Every LetterCrack puzzle is a famous quote in disguise. Each letter has been swapped for another. Work out the swaps, letter by letter, until the words appear.

A new quote every day
One daily cryptogram, the same for everyone. Every past daily waits in the archive.

Solve at your own pace
No timers. No lives. Wrong guesses are never judged until you ask. Leave and come back exactly where you were.

Meet the author
After every solve: the full quote, a short note on who wrote it and the work it comes from. Every quote is public domain.

Big, clear letters
A large keyboard, four text sizes, dark mode, high contrast and TalkBack support. Hardware keyboards work too.

Features
• Daily quote, a level path and an archive
• Four difficulty levels
• Reveal and Check hints
• Fill every matching letter at once, or one at a time
• Streaks with a weekly freeze, stats and favorite authors
• Share a solved quote as a card
